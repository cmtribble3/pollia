Main JIT kernel operation counts:

The operation counts are equal in these custom integrators. Their Jarecki arithmetic
runs in the integrator after querying material attributes, unlike stock Mitsuba path
which evaluates each material BSDF. Thus this experiment does not reproduce the large
graph-size reduction reported for stock path; similar IR counts do not imply identical
generated machine code or memory behavior.
